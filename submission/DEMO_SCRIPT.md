# Demo Script

## Setup (before you present)

- Open the game. Go to **Settings → Unlock all levels: On** and **Sound: On**. Go back to the title.
- Browser at 100% zoom, full screen (F11).
- Optional: keep a second tab on **Quantum Lab** for expert questions.

---

## The 90-second demo (no stopping the game)

| Time | On screen | You do | You say |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0–10 s** | Title: two orbs, the link between them changing shape | — | "Alice and Bob share something: two qubits, one pair. Our game is about sending two bits by posting one qubit." |
| **10–25 s** | Level 1 | `Space`, then `Q` | "Each half answers at random, but the pair always agrees. That's entanglement." *(The link snaps: asking used it up.)* |
| **25–40 s** | Levels map → Level 5 | Click **05 ONE QUBIT** | "Bob needs the message 11. He has nothing from me. One qubit." |
| | Lone qubit | `F`, `P`, `Space` | "FLIP, PHASE, send…" Bob reads **?1**. "One bit. PHASE never showed up on a lone qubit. That's Holevo's limit." |
| **40–50 s** | Part B | `Enter`, `S` | "Rewind to this morning, before any message existed. I SHARE a pair: Bob's half crosses, carrying nothing." *(A message 11 arrives.)* |
| **50–65 s** | Shaping | `F`, `P` | "FLIP crosses the link. PHASE makes it zigzag: ANTI. I only touched *my* half." `Space`: "One qubit crosses." |
| **65–80 s** | Analyzer + banner | — | "Bob's analyzer asks the *pair*: untangle, turn, read… **11.**" Banner: **ONLY ONE QUBIT CROSSED THE CHANNEL.** |
| **80–90 s** | Ledger + spy line | Point at the panel | "Honest accounting: Bob's half crossed earlier, when the channel was idle. And a spy grabbing my qubit reads 50/50 for every message. The qubit never held two bits. The pair did." |

**One-sentence close:** "Shared entanglement + one local move + one qubit + a joint measurement = two bits. That's superdense coding, and that's our game."

## The 3-minute demo

1. **(0:00–0:20) Hook.** Title, then Level 1: ask both ▲▼ twice. "Random, yet always equal."
2. **(0:20–0:50) Local move, joint change.** Level 3:
   - `F`: "only my half: the link crosses." `Q`: "opposite answers now."
   - `F`, then `O`: "Bob checks his half alone: still 50/50. He can't see my move. No faster-than-light anything."
3. **(0:50–1:20) Why a joint measurement.** Level 4:
   - `Q` then `W`: "I asked ▲▼, then ◀ ▶. The game flags the second answer as meaningless: the first question broke the link."
   - Guess wrong, then `A`: "The analyzer asks the pair: both bits."
4. **(1:20–2:10) The signature.** Level 5, exactly as in the 90 s demo.
5. **(2:10–2:45) Why it matters.** Level 7 (RUSH HOUR):
   - "The forecast shows when messages come, never what they say. I bank pairs in the quiet (`S` ×5)." Then play a few rush messages.
   - Optional: "Here's the same rush with entanglement switched off" (button on the clear card, or `#/level/7/classical`). It's lost.
6. **(2:45–3:00) Depth on demand.** Press `V` for CIRCUIT names. Open the Quantum Lab, preset `11`: "Ψ⁻, reduced states both I/2, CNOT then H then measure gives 11."
